2. Information Collected and Processed

A. Information Provided Directly

  • User Account Information: Name, email address, and authentication credentials when creating an account, signing in, or accessing member areas.
  • Support Inquiries: Contact information and the contents of any messages sent directly to customer support or feedback desks.

B. Technical and Diagnostic Data

  • Device Identifiers and Technical Details: Device model, operating system version, system build numbers, unique device identifiers, IP address, and network connection status.
  • Usage Telemetry: Video, podcast, and documentary playback history, saved bookmarks, article reading logs, and search queries within the application.
  • Crash and Performance Metrics: Application crash logs and technical performance traces used to diagnose bugs and maintain software stability.

3. Purpose of Processing Data

The collected data is utilized exclusively for legitimate business and operational purposes:

  • Authenticating registered accounts and managing user access.
  • Streaming high-definition videos, audio podcasts, documentaries, and written articles.
  • Maintaining user state, saved bookmarks, and custom watch lists.
  • Monitoring app uptime, resolving software errors, and reducing streaming buffering times.
  • Delivering administrative updates and content release announcements.
  • Safeguarding the platform against unauthorized access, scraping, fraud, and security threats.

4. Third-Party Disclosures

User information is never sold, leased, or traded to data brokers or third parties. Disclosures are limited to technical service providers under strict data-protection standards:

  • Cloud Infrastructure Providers: Secure hosting servers, content delivery networks (CDNs), and cloud databases.
  • Analytics and Diagnostic Providers: Analytics and crash-reporting software suites used solely for software performance metrics.
  • Statutory Compliance: Disclosure when strictly necessary to comply with legal processes, judicial warrants, or law enforcement mandates under applicable Indian statutes.

5. Data Security

Standard physical, administrative, and technical controls—including secure socket layer (SSL) and transport layer security (TLS) encryption for data transmitted between client devices and servers—are deployed to protect all stored information from unauthorized alteration, loss, or inspection.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Retention Period: Account profiles and saved preferences are preserved only for the duration the account remains active or as required by law.

In-App Account Deletion

Accounts can be deleted directly inside the application interface by selecting:

Profile > Settings > Account > Delete Account

Completing this process immediately initiates the permanent deletion of personal details, watch logs, and bookmarks.

Web-Based Deletion Request

Users who do not have the application installed or cannot access their login screen may submit a deletion request through our dedicated deletion page:

🗑️ Open Account & Data Deletion Page →

Alternatively, send an email directly to sgurukulamonline@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request - Sanathana Gurukulam". All verified requests will be permanently purged from active production servers within thirty (30) days.

7. Children's Information

Sanathana Gurukulam is an educational and cultural media platform intended for general audiences. The service does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under thirteen years of age without verifiable parental consent. If personal data from a child has been gathered inadvertently, that record will be erased upon discovery.
